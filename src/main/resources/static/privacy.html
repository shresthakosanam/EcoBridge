Privacy Policy

Prototype notice: This policy is placeholder text for a college or hackathon prototype and requires professional legal review before commercial use.

EcoBridge receives your Google account ID, name, email, and profile image through Google OpenID Connect. It stores these with pickups, event memberships, posts, comments, and likes. Firebase Storage processes uploaded images; PostgreSQL stores application data in production.

Pickup addresses and schedules are returned only to the signed-in owner. Do not submit sensitive information in public posts or event descriptions.
