Privacy Policy

Prototype notice: This policy is placeholder text and requires professional legal review before commercial use.

EcoBridge receives your Google account ID, name, email, and profile image through Google OpenID Connect. It stores account and application records in MySQL. Firebase Storage processes uploaded image files; MySQL stores only their URLs and metadata.

Pickup addresses and schedules are returned only to the signed-in owner and authorized collectors. Do not submit sensitive information in public posts or event descriptions.
